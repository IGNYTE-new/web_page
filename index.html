Objection Handling

The "I need to think about it" Crusher

Use this when a customer goes ghost after getting the finance quote.

Act as a sympathetic but persistent car sales expert. Write a short, 2-sentence follow-up email to a customer who stopped replying after receiving a finance quote for a [Insert Car Model]. Acknowledge it's a big decision, but subtly remind them that this stock moves fast. End with a low-pressure question about their preferred monthly budget.
Inbound Response

The "Is this still available?" Reply

Turn a generic Facebook marketplace query into a phone call.

Write a response to a Facebook Marketplace enquiry asking "Is this available?". 1. Confirm yes. 2. Create urgency (mention 2 other viewings). 3. Ask "Are you looking to finance or cash?" to qualify them immediately. Keep it under 50 words. Informal tone.
